Case studies / Manitoba outreach

Building a B2B lead-generation pathway for Manitoba's creative industries

Moonlite Labs · B2B marketing and lead generation · October 2026

I developed industry-specific email outreach for Manitoba businesses, connecting tailored messaging with a direct opportunity to book a meeting with the sales team.

01 / The brief

A commercial objective, two audiences

Moonlite Labs wanted to reach potential business customers in Manitoba and create opportunities for sales conversations. The target sectors were film and television, and advertising and marketing. Both work with visual content, but their priorities and production contexts differ.

The challenge was to make the outreach relevant to each sector and give interested recipients a straightforward next step, rather than rely on a generic product introduction.

02 / Audience strategy

Segment first, then shape the message

I developed separate email campaigns for the two industries so that the positioning and language could reflect their different professional contexts. The segmentation was a messaging decision, not simply a way to divide a mailing list.

AUDIENCE 01

Film & television

Dedicated outreach framed for film and television businesses and their production context.

AUDIENCE 02

Advertising & marketing

Separate outreach adapted for advertising, agency and marketing-oriented work.

03 / Lead-generation design

A clear route from outreach to sales

Each campaign included a meeting-booking link connected to the sales team. Instead of ending with a general invitation to explore the product, the email gave interested prospects a specific way to request a conversation.

04 / Measured response

Email engagement

The October campaigns recorded click rates ranging from 6% to 12%, the strongest email click-rate results reported that year in the available campaign summary.

My contribution

Audience segmentation, industry-specific campaign messaging, email execution and performance analysis, with a meeting-booking call to action connecting marketing outreach to sales.
