Case studies / Website and Inspiration

Making an AI platform easier to explore

Moonlite Labs · Brand communications, content strategy & audience experience · May–October 2026

I led the content and brand direction for Moonlite Labs' website relaunch and conceived its Inspiration page as a resource that helps visitors understand and explore creative possibilities.

Audience experience#2Inspiration page ranked second in site visits after the homepage in the latest measured period through 3 October 2026

01Challenge

Moonlite Labs needed a clearer way to explain an expanding creative AI platform to filmmakers, agencies, brands and other professional users. Visitors needed to understand what the product could help them do and find relevant examples and guidance.

02My role

As the first dedicated marketing, communications and partnerships professional, I formalized the company's brand identity, tone of voice, messaging guidelines and reusable standards. I shaped the website's content architecture and visitor journeys, rewrote and structured content, and coordinated the relaunch with the teams responsible for implementation.

03Strategy and execution

I reviewed the existing site against audience needs, business priorities and the new brand framework. I developed a multi-page prototype to clarify the proposed structure, coordinated implementation and created the company blog as an ongoing information channel.

I also conceived, proposed and implemented the Inspiration page, using examples, reusable prompts and before-and-after editing scenarios to help visitors discover practical ways to use the platform. I continue to own its content and editorial direction.

I subsequently expanded the site's product education and information architecture with new Team Accounts help content, an MCP product page, and a detailed article explaining how to use Moonlite Labs within Claude and ChatGPT. I introduced an MCP announcement banner on the homepage and added MCP to the primary navigation to make the new capability easier to discover. I also built out the newsroom's press-release and media-resource sections, making company background information, executive biography and brand assets easier for journalists to find.

04Outcome

The redesigned website launched in July 2026, with the new brand and messaging applied throughout. The Inspiration page became the site's second-most-visited page after the homepage in the latest available measurement period through 3 October 2026. The blog, product pages, help resources and newsroom established additional ways for audiences to understand the platform, find information and follow company developments.

05Professional focus

This was work in brand stewardship, communication strategy, editorial planning and audience experience, carried out in coordination with technical implementation teams.
