About me

I am a marketing and communications professional with over ten years of experience in brand strategy, integrated marketing, corporate communications, public relations and stakeholder engagement. I've worked in startups and large organizations across the public and private sectors, with experience in energy, infrastructure, finance, IT, AI and media sectors.

My interdisciplinary background brings together analytical thinking, marketing strategy and digital media. I take a research-driven approach to storytelling, drawing on data, audience insights and industry knowledge to translate complex technical, financial and policy information into clear, compelling narratives. I also enjoy collaborating with technical teams and exploring emerging technologies.

I am drawn to purpose-driven work and bring purpose and intention to everything I do.

Outside work, I love world cinema, art and poetry. I enjoy painting and writing poetry, and I'm currently learning to play the piano (the piano and I are still getting acquainted!) and French.
