Case studies / Manitoba outreach

Targeted outreach to Manitoba business leaders

Moonlite Labs · B2B marketing & email strategy · October 2026

I developed segmented outreach to Manitoba's creative and business communities, adapting the message to how different organizations might use AI in their work.

Campaign click rates6%–12%Industry-segmented email sends, October 2026

01Challenge

A broad message about generative AI would not address the needs of every business. Film and television teams, advertising and marketing agencies, and other creative organizations have different production processes and reasons to explore new tools. Outreach needed to make the relevance of the platform clear to each audience.

02My role

As Moonlite Labs' Marketing, Communications & Partnerships Lead, I developed the audience approach, messaging and email communications, using campaign performance to evaluate response.

03Approach

I organized the outreach by industry and tailored the copy to each segment's workflows, from planning and visualizing ideas to producing and refining creative work. The messages invited relevant businesses to continue the conversation and explore practical applications.

04Results

The segmented campaigns achieved click rates ranging from 6% to 12%, demonstrating response to audience-specific messaging. These figures measure clicks, not meetings booked or sales conversions.

05What this demonstrates

The work brought together industry research, business-oriented messaging, audience segmentation and performance measurement. It illustrates my approach to making emerging technology relevant to specific professional audiences.
