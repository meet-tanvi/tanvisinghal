How I run social media

Strategy and content planning

I set content pillars around product launches, explainers, live event coverage, creator showcases and company news, and manage the content calendar.

Copywriting and thought leadership

I write post copy, captions, video scripts and content positioning the company within AI and filmmaking.

Creative direction

I brief and direct our Content Creator, then review and approve content before publishing.

Community management

I respond to comments and direct messages, keeping conversations timely and on brand.

Creator partnerships and UGC

I run creator partnerships and user-generated content campaigns on LinkedIn and Instagram.

Performance analysis

I track engagement, reach and click-through rate at the post level, share insights with the Founder and CEO and recommend changes to strategy and content mix.

Industry and competitor analysis

I monitor developments in AI and filmmaking and analyze competitors' content to sharpen our positioning.

Accessibility and compliance

I build captions, alt text, colour contrast and plain language into every post, and use original or properly licensed audio.

Social listening and reputation

At REC, I oversaw media monitoring and social listening through agencies and Hootsuite, and helped shape responses on reputation-sensitive issues.

Tools

Metricool, LinkedIn Analytics, Google Analytics, Klaviyo, Hootsuite, Canva and generative AI tools.
